# IN UTERO — Red Gray mythology and collectible experiences

**Approved creative and commercial concept — November 26, 2026. Not a delivered product or verified integration.**

IN UTERO introduces the Red Grays, an original fictional Martian culture descended from traditional Gray aliens: telepathic cybernetic biological beings who favor virtual existence, quiet underground habitats and scientific exploration. Their Planetary Almanacs are illustrated archives of life-bearing worlds. Their opposition to disruptive industrial activity and their fascination with marbles give them a recognizable cultural identity.

> Humanity lost its marbles on Earth. The Red Grays found theirs on Mars.

The project aims to develop an enduring fictional archetype, with the cultural recognizability of elves or vampires as an ambition rather than a prediction of market success.

## Proposed experiences

| Experience | Concept |
| --- | --- |
| Martian Marbles | In-game discovery, collecting, earning, trading and play under Martian gravity |
| Official physical marbles | Collectible sets, game bundles, rewards and physical trading |
| Living character cards | Holographic-style collectible cards featuring individual Red Gray personalities and favorite marbles, with a possible access mechanism for interactive AI |
| Planetary Almanacs | Illustrated storytelling, comics and expandable fictional archives |

## Potential infrastructure application

A physical card could act as an access point to a persistent virtual character, subject to appropriate permissions. Houston could coordinate signed requests and recovery, KyleDaemon could support memory, KDA Audio could provide voice, KZip could preserve durable records, and Vivian could supply spatial context where applicable. These are candidate integrations through existing SDKs and application-owned adapters, not claims of delivered card functionality. Product ownership and application authority remain separate.

Physical access tokens, identity, consent, transfer, revocation, privacy, manufacturing, fulfillment and commercial terms require further design and validation. No product availability, sales forecast or market demand is asserted. The independent infrastructure suite retains its broader purpose; IN UTERO is a creative reference application, not a limitation on its scope.
